**Rule**: place one keynote per fixture-with-keynote per yaml profile. Position the keynote ~1 ft from the fixture in a direction that doesn't conflict with other annotations (default: 1 ft north for counter-top receps, 1 ft east for cardio, 1 ft elevation-appropriate for others). Use a leader from the keynote pointing to the fixture for visual association.
